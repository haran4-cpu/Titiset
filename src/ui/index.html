FORGE

Two synthesized decks. Every track is composed from a genre contract — tempo, drum grammar, bass grammar, harmony, arrangement — and played by an audio-thread engine that times every hit to the sample. Mix by hand, or let Auto-DJ run an endless, phrase-aligned, key-compatible set.

New in 9.3 · real drum kits (808-, 909-, 707-, Linn-style, acoustic, lo-fi) with your own samples · chords that push and lag instead of always landing with the kick · Beat FX: delay, dub echo, reverb, filter, roll, brake and more, on the whole deck or one part · ten transition styles, Auto-DJ varies them · a phone-first layout: PLAY · MIX · FX · EDIT · SET.
