DJ TiTo 10 — listening guide (tools/listen_v10.cjs <outdir>)
==========================================================

Rendered by DJ TiTo's own engine and MP3 encoder (192 kb/s), with the palette and levels a new track gets. LEVELS.txt lists
length, loudness and true peak.

1. SPACE (space-<genre>-before / -after): the same 8 bars of a drop with the mix carve off (9.5) and on (10), six genres.
   Listen for the lead and the bass coming forward out of the chords. The "after" clips are 0.3–0.5 LU quieter (the carve
   takes energy out where parts collided): compare clarity, not volume.
2. BREAKDOWNS THAT BREATHE (breathe-*): no kick, the pads pump on the beat — trance, progressive house, melodic techno.
3. THE BASS STEPS OUT (predrop-*): the last 2 bars of a build and 2 of the drop; the bass is out for the build's last two beats.
4. TWO PARTS, TWO EFFECTS (parts-fx): a deep house drop, a delay on the bass from bar 3, a reverb on the chords from bar 5.

FORGE 9.5 — listening guide (the preview pack: tools/listen_v95a.cjs <outdir>)
=========================================================================

Rendered by FORGE's own engine and MP3 encoder (192 kb/s). LEVELS.txt lists length, loudness and true peak.

1. BUILDS INTO DROPS (build-1…8): the last 4 bars of a build and 2 of its drop, as the composer now writes them — a layered
   recipe in the key of the track, and on some drops an impact or a sub drop. Listen for: risers that belong to the track.
2. RECIPES ALONE (tx-*): six risers and downlifters, a sub drop, an impact, a swell — peak-normalised, for their shape.
3. MANGLE (mangle-*): 2 bars of a drop as written, then 4 with one engine on. Listen for: in time, in key, no clicks.
4. EAR CANDY (candy-*-new / -old): each rebuilt sound next to the 9.4 sound it replaces, on the same 4 bars of a drop.

FORGE 9.4 — listening guide
===========================

All files are rendered by FORGE's own engine (the same code the app plays) and encoded by FORGE's own MP3 encoder at
320 kb/s — so the pack is also a test of the encoder. Length, loudness and true peak of every file are in LEVELS.txt,
with the sounds each track drew. Best on headphones or good speakers. Nothing here is a sample of another record or machine.

1. TRACKS — four new tracks of one genre  (tracks-<genre>-1…4.mp3)
   melodic techno, deep house, psytrance, drum & bass: the first drop (8 bars) of four tracks each.
   In 9.3 four tracks of a genre shared most of their sounds. In 9.4 each draws its own lead, chords, pad, bass, arp and
   often its own kit family, from the genre's palette across the eight new engines, varied for that track.
   Listen for: four tracks of a genre that sound like four records, not four takes of one.

2. KITS — one groove, six new kit families  (kit-<family>.mp3)
   The same tech-house drop, the same notes, only the kit family changes:
     kit-cr78        CR-78-style soft analog: short "tss" hats, a popping snare, blips for the hand drums, no hard edges
     kit-minimal     dry and clipped: every voice shortened, thinner snares, nothing rings
     kit-industrial  metal and distortion: plates under the snare, folded hats, an anvil for a cowbell, a big hard room
     kit-vintage     a break from an old record: a bigger room, the kit pushed into tape, a narrower band
     kit-trap        808-style, but tight: very bright hats, a snapping clap, a crisp snare
     kit-garage      crisp and short: skippy hats with a little shaker in them, a high snappy snare, a woody rim

3. BUILDS — builds of different shapes into their drops  (build-<shape>-<genre>.mp3)
   The last 8 bars of a build and the first 4 of its drop. Each build combines a drum shape, an effect and an ending
   (LEVELS.txt names all three). In 9.3 every build carried the same noise riser.

4. LEAD 2 — a second melodic voice  (lead2-<mode>-<genre>.mp3)
     answer   speaks in the lead's rests (call and response)
     counter  a counter-line below the lead, in longer notes
     motif    where the genre has no lead: a motif over the drop

5. LENGTH AND EXTEND  (full-melodic-techno.mp3, extended-melodic-techno.mp3)
     full-…      one whole melodic-techno track at the Club length (the form is in LEVELS.txt)
     extended-…  the same track from 8 bars before the end of its last drop, with Extend pressed there: one more act
                 in the track's own style, then its outro. Nothing before the splice changes.

What to send back: tracks of one genre that still sound too alike; sounds you would never want (name the file and the
part — LEVELS.txt lists the presets); builds or LEAD 2 lines that feel wrong; whether Club length is right for you.
